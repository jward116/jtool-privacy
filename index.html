jtool and Google Drive Manager

jtool and Google Drive Manager are personal tools Justin Ward uses to organize and search his own Google Drive. They are not offered to anyone else.

Privacy

The tools work only on the Google account that signs in to them. They keep their working copy of file information on Justin's own computer, and they never sell it or give it to other people.

Access can be removed at any time from the Google account's third-party access page.
